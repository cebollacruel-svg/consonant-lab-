Ten short units. Each one gives you a two-minute review — how the sound is made and how it is spelled — and then four drills where you identify the sound. No clues during the drills.

How each unit works

  1. 1

    Review

    Read the card, use a mirror, touch your throat to feel the voicing. Two minutes, no test.

  2. 2

    Minimal pairs

    You hear one word. Choose which of the two it was — nothing is marked for you.

  3. 3

    In a sentence

    The same contrast, but inside natural speech, where the word is not isolated.

  4. 4

    Sound sorting

    Say the word yourself and decide which sound it has. The spelling will try to trick you.

  5. 5

    Spelling rules

    Given a spelling pattern, choose the sound. This is what transfers to new words.
